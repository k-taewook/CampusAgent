A B S T R A C T
In addition to the information society, the 4th Industrial Revolution craze, and smart phones are also
emerging as one of the important items of society, affecting many parts of our lives. Videos and games
that parents show to their young children in places like restaurants through smart-phones do not help
children's emotional stability and development. This study focuses on coloring especially in mobile apps
to help children's emotional stability and development.
The proposed coloring app consists of two stages. First stage is the process by which parents
understand their children's current emotional state. In the case of infants, the parents understand the
psychological state of the infant, and in the case of a child who knows the writing, the child's
psychological state is identified through inquiry and classified into five categories. The second is to
provide the color and sketch according to the psychological state and to play the coloring.
In this study, we investigated emotional development theory and developed prototyping application
based on children 's emotion using color psychology and artificial intelligence inference technology on
Android OS.
